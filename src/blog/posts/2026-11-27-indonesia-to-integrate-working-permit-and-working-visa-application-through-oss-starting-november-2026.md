**Overview of the New Regulation**

Based on the [Joint Ministerial Decision No. 1 of 2026](https://jdih.kemnaker.go.id/peraturan/detail/3097/keputusan-bersama-menteri-ketenagakerjaan-menteri-imigrasi-dan-pemasyarakatan-dan-menteri-investasi-dan-hilirisasi-kepala-badan-koordinasi-penanaman-modal-nomor-1-tahun-2026), issued on September 9, 2026 by the Ministry of Manpower, the Ministry of Immigration and Corrections, and the Ministry of Investment and Downstream Industry/BKPM, Indonesia is introducing a new integrated system for foreign worker (TKA) licensing.

As announced in TKA Online platform, transition on procedures for working permit and working visa application has begun since November 23, 2026.

**Previous Foreign Worker Permit and Visa Application Procedures**

Previously, working permit in the form of[ *Hasil Penilaian Kelayakan* and *Pengesahan RPTKA*](https://www.javasahevaraya.com/visa-kerja-keluarga) application for foreign workers were processed through [TKA Online](https://tka-online.kemnaker.go.id/) system by Ministry of Manpower.

Once the required working permit have been issued, employers will need to proceed through separate Immigration platform, namely [Molina/Visa Online](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/). 

The integration between the two systems beforehand was mainly facilitated through the foreign worker permit registration number, which was required for the subsequent visa application process.

The new integration introduces a more comprehensive connection between the both system for streamlining the process.

**What Changes Under the New OSS Integration?**

Through the new integration, [OSS (Online Single Submission)](https://oss.go.id/id) will serve as single entry point for working permit and working visa application.

The system integrated [SIAPKerja](https://siapkerja.kemnaker.go.id/) platform by Ministry of Manpower, and also [All Indonesia](https://allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id/) by Ministry of Immigration and Corrections.

Through this integration, employers can monitor and proceed the relevant foreign worker licensing application through one integrated platform rather than jumping to applications separately across different government systems.

However, every ministry will continue to handle their own assessment, verification, and approval processes through their integrated systems. 

**What Does This Mean for Companies Employing Foreign Workers?**

With OSS serving as the single entry point, employers can expect a more streamlined application process for foreign worker employment and immigration-related approvals.

Nevertheless, companies should pay attention to the transition period, particularly regarding applications that will be processed under the new system.

Further technical guidance and operational details may become clearer during the implementation of the new system.
